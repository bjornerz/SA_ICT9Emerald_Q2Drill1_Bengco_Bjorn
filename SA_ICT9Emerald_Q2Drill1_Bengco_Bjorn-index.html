A Guide to Volleyball



Volleyball is a popular team sport where two teams of six players use their hands to bat a ball back and forth over a high net. The primary objective is to ground the ball within the opponent's court before they can return it. Teams are limited to three hits to send the ball over the net, and no player may hit the ball twice in succession, except in certain situations allowed by the rules.

Rules
  • Maximum of Three Hits per Side: Each team is allowed up to three touches to return the ball over the net, excluding a block touch.
  • No Catching, Holding, or Throwing: The ball must be hit cleanly and cannot be caught, held, or thrown.
  • Six Players and Team Rotation: Each team has six players on the court, with three in the front row and three in the back row. Players rotate clockwise when their team wins back the serve.
  • Scoring: A team usually wins a set by reaching 25 points with at least a two-point lead.
Basic Skills
  • Serving: Putting the ball into play by hitting it over the net.
  • Passing: Receiving the ball and directing it toward a teammate.
  • Setting: Positioning the ball for a teammate to attack.
  • Spiking: Hitting the ball forcefully into the opponent's court.
Player Positions
  • Setter: Organizes the team's attacks by setting up teammates.
  • Outside Hitter: Attacks from the left side and helps receive serves.
  • Middle Blocker: Blocks attacks and performs quick hits near the net.
  • Libero: Specializes in defensive plays and receiving serves.
